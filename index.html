Transmutation table & options

Starting SY 2026–2027, DepEd Order No. 15, s. 2026 uses an Adjusted Transmutation Table where an Initial Grade of 70.00 converts to a passing 75 (previously 60 → 75). The band edges below were recalibrated directly against 36 real Initial Grade → Term Grade pairs found in the shared class record, since a secondary web source used earlier produced a couple of mismatches (e.g. 74.50 was showing 78 instead of the correct 76). Every band now has at least one confirmed match from that sheet. Bands with no example in the sheet — mostly Initial Grades below ~60, where few or no students scored — are interpolated and are the least certain part of this table. If you spot another mismatch against your official copy, share the Initial Grade and Term Grade shown and this can be tightened further.
